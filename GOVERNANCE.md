# Governance

Weavers of the Void is developed in the open and led by its maintainers. This file says who decides what, how contributions are judged, and how the project pays for itself.

## Who decides

- **The project owner** is the copyright holder named in [NOTICE](NOTICE). The owner holds the marks, appoints maintainers and makes the final technical and architectural decisions.
- **Maintainers** review and merge contributions, triage issues, cut releases and keep the rules below. The current maintainers are listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
- **Contributors** are everyone who sends an issue, a discussion, a review or a pull request.

Decisions are made in public whenever they can be: in issues, pull requests and the roadmap. Security matters and conduct reports are handled privately.

## How contributions are judged

Anyone may open an issue or a pull request. A contribution is merged when it:

- passes the project's checks and follows the engineering rules in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md);
- is sound in design, quality and security;
- fits the direction of the project;
- is signed off under the [Developer Certificate of Origin](DCO).

A maintainer may decline a contribution that does not meet these points and says why. The project owner settles disagreements that the maintainers cannot.

## The roadmap

The roadmap is public. Proposals for features, expansions and mechanics are welcome as issues. The maintainers decide what is built and in what order.

## Licensing stays as it is

The software is licensed under the AGPL-3.0, with the exceptions listed in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md). Every published version keeps the rights its license grants, whatever happens later. Contributions come in under the license of the files they change (inbound equals outbound), and the project asks for no copyright assignment and no contributor license agreement.

## How the project pays for itself

The software is free and stays complete: there is no paid edition, no locked feature and no license fee. The project funds itself through services that people may choose to buy: custom worldbuilding, custom universe engineering, canon management, consulting and training, managed cloud hosting and enterprise deployment. Others may offer the same kinds of services, including hosting, under their own name (see [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md)).

- Sponsorship is voluntary. A sponsor receives no special rights, rewards, priority or say over the project.
- Contributors are not paid by the project for contributions, and no payment is promised.

## Contact

| Matter | Where |
|---|---|
| Bugs, features, questions | [GitHub issues](https://github.com/egedursun/weavers-of-the-void/issues) |
| Licensing questions | a GitHub issue with the `licensing` label |
| Trademark permission or misuse | a GitHub issue with the `trademark` label, or privately as below |
| Security vulnerabilities | privately, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) |
| Conduct reports and anything private | a private message to a maintainer listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) |

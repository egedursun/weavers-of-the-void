# Weavers of the Void®: Goals, Growth and Score

A world needs no winner to be worth living, but the game keeps count. Your cosmos earns a score, you can play toward goals and earn achievements, your sons grow in level, stars and rarity, and the Creator's own essence, mood and the doom clock rise and fall with what happens. This booklet sets out every part of that game and the numbers behind it.

| Achievements | 58, in 174 tiers |

## How to read this booklet

- Every number here is that of a new world. Each is a world rule, so it changes when you change the rules in Settings.
- The Goals screen shows your score, your goals and your achievements as they stand. Your sons' pages show their level, stars and rarity.
- The companion booklet *The Powers of a God* lists the powers that start goals, raise stars, lift a son to a higher rarity and set the doom clock by hand.

## Your score

Your score is counted again at the end of every epoch. It adds up these parts:

Population, Diversity of records, Languages, Worlds, Civilization, Life, Faith, Culture, Knowledge, Wonders, Scales of the cosmos, Exploration, Doomsday, Achievements and Goals.

Most parts count the things your cosmos holds, each up to a cap. The doom clock takes points away as it nears its threshold. Achievements and finished goals add the points they are worth.

## How your sons grow

### Rarity

Every son is a card of a rarity. The rarity sets its action points, how much essence it regains each epoch, the range of its values, the greatest power it may give its creations and how often it is drawn when you summon. A new world starts with Common, Rare and Legendary sons.

| Rarity | Action points | Essence each epoch | Values from | Values to | Power cap | Steps in a turn | Summon weight |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Common | 2 | 8 | 17 | 39 | 2 | 12 | 50 |
| Uncommon | 3 | 12 | 26 | 50 | 4 | 15 | 26 |
| Rare | 4 | 17 | 36 | 62 | 6 | 18 | 14 |
| Epic | 5 | 24 | 48 | 75 | 8 | 21 | 7 |
| Legendary | 6 | 32 | 61 | 88 | 10 | 24 | 3 |
| Mythic | 7 | 44 | 75 | 100 | 12 | 27 | never summoned |

### Level

A son gains experience and fame from its deeds and from reaching its aims. Each level asks for more experience than the last; at each level two of its values rise. Its powers awaken at levels 1, 3 and 5.

### Stars

You can give a son a star once it has the level and fame the star asks for; the essence comes from you. A son wears up to 5 stars, every star raises each of its values, and the highest star gives it an extra action.

## How your sons see you

Each son holds a view of you, made of love, fear and how long you have been silent toward it. Kind deeds raise its love, stern deeds its fear. The view decides how its loyalty and wrath drift each epoch.

| View | When | Loyalty each epoch | Wrath each epoch |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Forgotten | You have long been silent toward the son, and it neither loves nor fears you. | -2 | +0 |
| Hated | The son fears you, has little love left and little loyalty. | -2 | +3 |
| Respected | The son both loves and fears you. | +1 | +1 |
| Feared | The son fears you, without much love. | +0 | +2 |
| Loved | The son loves you, without much fear. | +2 | +0 |
| Distant | You have been silent toward the son for a long time. | -1 | +0 |
| Acknowledged | None of the above: the son knows you, and that is all. | +0 | +0 |

- **Kind deeds:** add star, answer prayer, ascend, bless essence, bless existence, bless hp, bless stat, forgive, grant power and resurrect son.
- **Stern deeds:** absolute command, chain, curse, demote, devour, drain essence, exile son, kill son, lightning and suppress rebellion.

## The doom clock

The shared clock of the cosmos. Godless communities, wars and curses advance it; miracles and faith push it back. At its threshold doom comes. The threshold is a world rule.

When doom comes, it takes one of the shapes listed under [Shapes of the end](world-terms.md#k-shapes-of-the-end) in *The Things of the World and Their Names*.

| Rule | In a new world |
|---|---:|
| Apocalypse threshold (apocalypse happens when the clock reaches this) | 100 |
| Essence gnawed by each godless community per epoch | 3 |
| Scenario: point the clock must not reach (% of threshold) | 80 |
| Score: penalty for the apocalypse when the clock is at the threshold | 150 |

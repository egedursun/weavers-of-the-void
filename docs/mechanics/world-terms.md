## How to read this booklet

- **Words of the game** explains the words the game and the other booklets use, with the Turkish name of each.
- **The kinds of things** are the things that have a page of their own in the game: you, your sons, foreign gods, worlds, stars and the rest.
- **The kinds of records** are the kinds of things that live in a world. Each has its values, the places it can live in and the tool a son makes it with.
- Ranges are those of a new world and follow your world's rules.
- Kinds a son cannot make are made by the world itself as it lives, and by you through the powers in *The Powers of a God*.

### Climate eras

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Temperate age** | Warmth and rain hold near their usual measure. |
| **Warming age** | Warmth rises step by step between two ages; frozen ground thaws. |
| **Cooling age** | Warmth falls step by step between two ages; ground freezes deeper. |
| **Drought century** | Less rain and more heat: rivers shrink and the dry inland suffers. |
| **Rain age** | More rain: floods on the coasts and plenty on the lowlands. |
| **Ice age** | Colder years: winters lengthen and high ground grows hard to cross. |
| **Scorching age** | The world grows hot: disease spreads more often. |
| **Hothouse age** | Heat and rain both rise far above their measure: cold lands thaw and the warm lands grow hard to bear. |
| **Snowball age** | Cold reaches nearly every land and rain falls little: only the warmest places stay open. |
| **Variable age** | The climate changes again within an epoch or two, so no season can be counted on. |
| **Wet and cold age** | Cooler years with more rain: fields lie wet and growing seasons shorten. |

### Shapes of the end

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Ruin** | The leakiest world is shaken by disasters. |
| **World war** | The end comes as a war of every people. |
| **Outer descent** | Outer beings descend on the leakiest world. |
| **End of the gods** | The sons fade twice as fast. |
| **Dimensional collapse** | Every veil gives way and the dimensions run together. |
| **Galactic death** | The stars of the dimmest galaxy go dark. |

### Lasting afflictions

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Venom** | A venom that rots the flesh where it enters, then leaves a lasting weakness. |
| **Drink dependence** | A need for strong drink that turns to withdrawal when it is denied. |
| **Drug dependence** | A hunger for a substance, with withdrawal when the supply fails. |
| **Beast curse** | Under the full face of the world's first moon the bearer becomes a thinking beast; a bite passes the curse on. |
| **Blood curse** | The bearer stops ageing, hungers for blood and burns under the sun; a feeding can pass it on. |
| **Divine blessing** | A god's favour: strength beyond the mortal measure, and a fear of the giver. |
| **Void taint** | A touch from outside that grows into madness and ends in the service of an outer cult. |
| **Possession** | A bound spirit rides the bearer and speaks with their mouth. |
| **Trauma** | A memory too heavy to carry that returns in fear and grief. |
| **Hunger curse** | A god's curse of a hunger that no meal fills. |
| **Warping** | What long exposure to magic does to a body and a mind. |
| **Vis dependence** | A body that has come to need magic to keep its years. |
| **Geas doom** | The doom that falls on one who broke a geas. |
| **Fortune** | A lasting shift of luck, for good or for ill, that holds and then fades; a line's fortune passes to its children. |
| **Transformation** | A change of the body into another plan, kind or substance, lasting or coming back at a set sign. |
| **Charm** | A compelled bond of love, obedience or devotion toward a person or a god, which wears off unless renewed. |
| **Soul trapped** | A soul held in a mythic realm while its body lies asleep, until another quest brings it back. |
| **Ageing** | Ageing sped, slowed or stopped for its holder, at a rate set when it is laid. |
| **Forgetting** | Memories, skills or a person's name taken from the holder, which can be learned again. |
| **Sleep** | A sleep the holder cannot leave until its cure comes. |

# Trademark Policy

The code of Weavers of the Void is free under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The name is not. This policy explains how the name "Weavers of the Void", its logo and its visual identity (together, "the marks") may be used. The marks are owned by the copyright holder named in [NOTICE](NOTICE).

The policy has one purpose: people must be able to tell the official project apart from forks and from other people's services. It never restricts what the software licenses allow you to do with the code. You may always run, study, change, fork, sell and host the software; this policy only governs the name and the logo you do it under.

## Always allowed, without asking

- Using the name to refer to the project truthfully: writing about it, reviewing it, teaching it, comparing it, linking to it.
- Saying truthfully that your product, fork or service is "based on Weavers of the Void", "compatible with Weavers of the Void" or "a fork of Weavers of the Void", in plain text and no more prominently than your own name.
- Running an unmodified official release, privately or for your organisation, with the name and logo as shipped.
- Redistributing an unmodified official release with the name and logo as shipped.
- Showing the logo next to a link to the official project, in articles, talks and lists of tools.
- Keeping the project's mark in the books, Codices and packages the software made for you. Your worlds are yours; see [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

## Allowed with your own name

You may publish a modified version or offer the software as a hosted service, commercially or not, at your own prices. When you do:

- Give it your own name and logo. Replace the name and logo in the interface (`frontend/src/config/brand.ts`, `backend/src/aerona/constants/app.py`, `frontend/public/brand/`, `backend/src/aerona/services/books/brand/`).
- Do not call it "Weavers of the Void", "Weavers of the Void Cloud", "WoV Cloud", "official" or any name that could be mistaken for the project.
- Do not suggest that the project endorses, certifies, supports or is affiliated with you.
- You may describe it truthfully as based on or compatible with Weavers of the Void, as above.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Using the marks as, or as part of, the name of a product, service, company, domain name or social media account.
- Using the name "WoV Cloud", "Weavers of the Void Cloud" or anything that presents a hosted service as the official one.
- Using a logo or visual identity that could be confused with the project's.
- Selling merchandise with the marks.
- Changing the logo, or combining it with another mark.

## Asking for permission

Open an issue with the `trademark` label, or contact the maintainers as described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#contact), and describe what you plan. Permission is given in writing and can be withdrawn if the use stops following this policy.

## Reporting misuse

If you see a product or service that may confuse people about what is official, tell the maintainers the same way. Reports are kept confidential.
